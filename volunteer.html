Student Teaching English Program

President (Grades 11–12) · Secretary (Grade 10) · Member (Grades 7–9)

September 2018 – June 2024

Ran weekly Wednesday meetings for 60+ members and connected the club with local schools and communities to host events. Organized biannual overnight teaching camps and seasonal kindergarten and elementary teaching trips, started letter exchanges with local students, and introduced biweekly tutoring sessions with non-native English speakers. Worked closely to support three special needs students and helped build members' Chinese speaking and teaching abilities. Won the 24th Taishin Youth Volunteer Elite Award for Community Service.
